Website Development

Building fast-loading, responsive websites, suitable for both mobile and desktop, through a "website-as-a-service" model. This would include a set-up fee and a monthly fee covering maintenance, hosting and small requested changes while you focus on your business or studies.

What kind of website is this best for?

This is best for smaller simple websites, like those for small businesses, portfolios, etc. They normally have less than 10 large pages (but can be more, it really depends on the project)

What is "website-as-a-service"?

The model, "website-as-a-service" includes an initial set-up fee alongside a monthly fee covering maintenance, hosting and small requested changes. These changes include some image changes, small information changes but not large restructuring of the entire website; those larger requests can be done, however it will be at an extra cost based on my hourly rate.

What are the benefits?

Due to what I make the websites out of, they end up being extremely fast-loading and responsive, as well as the beautiful designs being perfectly suited for mobile devices too.

What do my services include?

Custom, or templated website design - depending on client preference - with hosting, maintenance and requested changes included in a monthly fee. Large changes aren't included, but can be done at an added cost. This does not include domain name management, however I will be happy to assist in walking clients through the process.

What does the whole process look like?

→ Half of the website set-up fee paid upfront.
→ Website developed.
→ Domain Registration.
→ The other half of the set-up fee paid.
→ Website goes live.
→ Monthly fee recurringly paid.

How much does it cost?

There is a two part cost for the main website alongside a small annual fee for the domain (normally <£10). Here is what they are:
→ £198 Set-up fee - £99 upfront & £99 before the website goes live.
→ <£10 Annual domain registration fee.
→ £35 Monthly maintenance & hosting fee.
