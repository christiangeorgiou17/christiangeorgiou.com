Website Development

I design and build fast, responsive websites that work seamlessly across desktop and mobile devices. My approach combines clean design, strong usability, and performance-focused development to create websites that are both polished and practical for business, portfolio, or personal use.

What kind of website is this best for?

This service is ideal for smaller businesses, personal brands, portfolios, and straightforward service-based websites. Most projects typically involve fewer than 10 major pages, though this can vary depending on the scope and requirements.

What is a website-as-a-service model?

The website-as-a-service model includes an initial setup fee alongside a recurring monthly fee that covers hosting, maintenance, and minor updates. This helps clients keep their website running smoothly without the need to manage day-to-day technical upkeep themselves.

What are the benefits?

Each website is built with speed, responsiveness, and clarity in mind. The result is a streamlined digital presence that looks professional, loads quickly, and performs well on mobile devices.

What is included in the service?

Depending on your preferences, I can deliver a fully custom design or a refined template-based build. The package includes hosting, maintenance, and requested updates within the agreed monthly support structure. Larger structural changes can be handled as additional work at an agreed hourly rate.

What does the process look like?
  1. 50% of the setup fee paid upfront.
  2. Website design and development.
  3. Domain registration and setup.
  4. Remaining 50% of the setup fee due before launch.
  5. Website goes live.
  6. Recurring monthly fee for maintenance and hosting.
How much does it cost?

The main website package includes a setup fee and an ongoing monthly support fee, with a separate annual domain fee typically under £10. The structure is as follows:

  1. £198 setup fee — £99 upfront and £99 before launch.
  2. Annual domain registration fee — typically under £10.
  3. £35 per month for hosting and maintenance.
